Before you can create an event on HackerSquad, you must have an approved company connected to your account.

![Company Portal](/images/company-portal.png)

## Before you create an event

Event creation is only available for approved companies on HackerSquad. If you do not have a company yet, create one first at [Create a company](https://hackersquad.io/companies/access-denied).

![Make a company](/images/apply-as-company.png)

After you submit your company, the HackerSquad team reviews and verifies it. You must wait until that review is complete before you can create events.

<Note>
  If your company is still pending review, you will not be able to create an event yet.
</Note>

## How event creation works

To create a company navigate to the events page on the left navbar. 

![Go to event navbar](/images/company-portal-event-navbar.png)

1. Create your company on HackerSquad.
2. Wait for the HackerSquad team to review and verify your company.
3. Once your company is verified, return to HackerSquad and create your event.

## If you are blocked

If you already created a company but still cannot create an event, your company may still be waiting for verification. Check your company status and wait for the HackerSquad team to complete the review.
