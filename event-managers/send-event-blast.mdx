---
title: "Send event blast"
description: "Send in-product updates to builders so they can quickly find resources and important event information on HackerSquad."
---

Blasts make it easy to send information to builders quickly during an event. They are a useful way to point builders to resources, reminders, and updates that are available directly inside HackerSquad.

Blasts do not send email. Instead, they help you notify builders inside the platform so they can find what they need without leaving the event experience.

## Why blasts matter

Blasts are especially helpful when you want to quickly direct builders to something important during the hackathon.

For example, you might use a blast to:

- Share a resource page
- Point builders to event instructions
- Announce that new information is available on HackerSquad
- Remind attendees where to find key links during the event

This works well alongside live announcements. For example, an event host can mention on the mic that a blast was just sent with resources builders can open on HackerSquad.

## Send a blast from event management

Event managers can send blasts from the event management tool.

![Send blast tool](/images/send-blast-tool.png)

Use this area to create and send short updates to the builders in your event.

<Tip>
Keep blasts short and action-oriented so builders can immediately understand what changed and where they should go next.
</Tip>

## Where builders see blasts

Builders see blasts in the builder dashboard.

![Builder dashboard blast](/images/builder-dashboard-blast.png)

Because blasts appear inside the event experience, they are a strong way to reinforce directions you already shared in person, in chat, or during kickoff.

## Best practices

Use blasts when you want builders to take a clear action right away.

Good blast messages usually:

- Tell builders what is available
- Explain why it matters
- Point them to the right place in HackerSquad
- Stay short enough to scan quickly
