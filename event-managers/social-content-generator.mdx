The Social Content Generator helps your DevRel team create targeted social media posts by ingesting your existing content — documentation, blog posts, changelogs — and using AI to generate platform-specific posts from that material.

## How it works

The workflow has three stages:

1. **Ingest** — Submit a URL to crawl and extract content
2. **Library** — Browse and manage your ingested content assets
3. **Generate** — Create AI-powered social posts from your content library

## Content ingestion

To add content to your library:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Social Content Generator">
    Navigate to the Social Content Generator from your [Company Dashboard](/company-portal/dashboard) or the Content & Contracts section in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a URL">
    Enter the URL of the page you want to ingest (e.g., your API documentation, a blog post, or a changelog entry). The system uses Firecrawl to crawl and extract the page content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for processing">
    The content is crawled, cleaned, and broken into chunks for the AI to reference. Processing typically takes a few seconds depending on page length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View in your library">
    Once processed, the content appears in your **Content Library** as a new asset ready for post generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Ingest your API documentation, changelog, or blog posts first, then generate targeted social content from that material.</Tip>

## Content library

The Content Library displays all of your ingested content assets. For each asset you can see:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Source URL** | The original URL that was crawled |
| **Chunks** | The number of content chunks extracted from the page |
| **Generated Posts** | How many social posts have been created from this asset |

You can delete assets you no longer need from the library. Deleting an asset does not remove posts that were already generated from it.

## AI post generation

To generate a social media post:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a platform">
    Choose the target social media platform. Each platform has different formatting and character limits:

    | Platform | Character Limit | Style |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Twitter** | 280 characters | Concise with hashtags |
    | **LinkedIn** | 3,000 characters | Professional and detailed |
    | **Bluesky** | 300 characters | Conversational and engaging |
    | **Threads** | 500 characters | Casual storytelling |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a custom prompt">
    Provide a prompt to guide the AI. For example: "Write a post announcing our new REST API endpoint for user authentication" or "Create a thread highlighting the top 3 features from this changelog."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the post">
    Click **Generate** to create the post. The AI uses your ingested content library as context and produces a platform-formatted post based on your prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and copy">
    Review the generated post. You can copy it directly to your clipboard for pasting into the target platform, or generate another version with a different prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Post management

All generated posts are saved in your **Post Library**. From here you can:

- **View** any previously generated post
- **Copy** post content to your clipboard
- **Delete** posts you no longer need
