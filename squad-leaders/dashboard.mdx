The Squad Leader dashboard is your home base on HackerSquad. It gives you a quick overview of your program status, key metrics, and shortcuts to the tools you use most.

## Program overview

At the top of the dashboard, you'll see a summary of your Squad Leader program status:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Events managed | Total number of events you've created and managed |
| Upcoming events | Events that haven't happened yet |
| Total earnings | Cumulative revenue earned from sponsorship commissions |
| Active sponsors | Number of sponsors currently engaged across your events |

## Quick links

The dashboard provides direct access to the key areas of your Squad Leader portal:

| Link | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Events](/squad-leaders/events) | View and create events |
| [Revenue](/squad-leaders/revenue) | Track your earnings and commission details |
| [Sponsor CRM](/squad-leaders/sponsor-crm) | Manage your sponsor prospect pipeline |
| [Agreements](/squad-leaders/agreements) | View and sign contracts |

## Personal statistics

The dashboard displays your personal performance metrics to help you track your growth as a Squad Leader:

- **Events hosted** — The total number of events you've run since joining the program
- **Sponsorship deals closed** — How many sponsors you've brought to your events
- **Total revenue generated** — The total sponsorship revenue across all your events (before platform fee)
- **Your earnings** — Your commission share (typically 70%) of total sponsorship revenue

<Tip>Check your dashboard regularly to stay on top of upcoming events and pending actions like unsigned agreements or sponsor follow-ups.</Tip>

## Getting started from the dashboard

If you're new to the Squad Leader program, the dashboard helps you get oriented:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review your program status">
    Confirm your Squad Leader application has been approved and your profile is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the quick links">
    Familiarize yourself with each section — Events, Revenue, Sponsor CRM, and Agreements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first event">
    Head to [Events](/squad-leaders/events) to create and configure your first event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start prospecting sponsors">
    Open the [Sponsor CRM](/squad-leaders/sponsor-crm) to begin building your sponsor pipeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>
