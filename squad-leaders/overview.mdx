Squad Leaders are community organizers who run HackerSquad events in their region. As a Squad Leader, you build and grow a local developer community, organize events, connect with sponsors, and earn revenue from sponsorship deals.

## What you can do

As a Squad Leader on HackerSquad, you can:

- **Run events** — Create and manage hackathons, hack nights, workshops, and more in your region using the full [Event Management](/event-management/overview) suite
- **Find sponsors** — Use the built-in [Sponsor CRM](/squad-leaders/sponsor-crm) to track prospects and close sponsorship deals
- **Earn revenue** — Receive a commission on every sponsorship deal you close, tracked in your [Revenue](/squad-leaders/revenue) dashboard
- **Manage agreements** — Handle contracts and e-signatures with sponsors through the [Agreements](/squad-leaders/agreements) system
- **Track payouts** — Monitor your earnings and payout history in the [Payouts](/squad-leaders/payouts) section
- **Build your brand** — Set up a public [Squad Leader profile](/squad-leaders/profile-setup) to showcase your community leadership
- **Access resources** — Get event planning guides, marketing templates, and best practices from the [Resources](/squad-leaders/resources) hub

## How it works

The Squad Leader program is built on a revenue-share model. When you bring sponsors to your events, you earn a commission on each sponsorship deal — typically **70% of sponsorship revenue** goes to you, with 30% as a platform fee.

| Component | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Commission rate | 70% (default) |
| Platform fee | 30% |
| Payment | Per sponsorship, tracked in your dashboard |
| Event access | Full event management suite |

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply to the program">
    Submit your [Squad Leader application](/squad-leaders/application) with details about your experience, region, and community vision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved">
    The HackerSquad team reviews your application. You'll be notified by email when a decision is made.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your profile">
    Create your public [Squad Leader profile](/squad-leaders/profile-setup) with a bio, photo, region, and social links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan your first event">
    Use the [Events](/squad-leaders/events) section to create and configure your first event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find sponsors">
    Start building your sponsor pipeline with the [Sponsor CRM](/squad-leaders/sponsor-crm) and close your first deal.
  </Step>
</Steps>
