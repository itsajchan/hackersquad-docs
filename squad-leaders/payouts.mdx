The Payouts page shows you the status of your commission payments. After you earn revenue from sponsorships, HackerSquad processes payouts so you receive your commission share.

## Payout statuses

Each payout goes through a defined lifecycle:

| Status | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Your commission has been earned but the payout hasn't been initiated yet |
| **Processing** | The payout is being processed and payment is on its way |
| **Paid** | The payment has been completed and funds have been transferred |

<Note>Payouts are processed after sponsorship payments have been confirmed and cleared. Processing times may vary.</Note>

## Payout history

Your payout history shows a record of all payments you've received:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Payout date | When the payout was issued |
| Amount | The total payout amount |
| Status | Current payout status (Pending, Processing, Paid) |
| Payment method | How the payment was sent |
| Related sponsorships | The sponsorship earnings included in this payout |

## Tracking your payouts

You can check the Payouts page at any time to see:

- **Pending payouts** — Commissions earned that haven't been paid out yet
- **In-progress payouts** — Payments currently being processed
- **Completed payouts** — Your full payment history with dates and amounts

<Tip>Check your Payouts page regularly to confirm payments have been received. If a payout remains in **Processing** for an extended period, contact support at inquiry@hackersquad.io.</Tip>
