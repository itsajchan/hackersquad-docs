The Resources section provides educational materials and practical tools to help you plan successful events, attract sponsors, and grow your local developer community.

## Event planning guides

Get step-by-step guidance for running different types of events:

- **Hack Night planning** — How to organize and run a focused evening hacking session
- **Hackathon planning** — End-to-end guide for multi-day hackathons including venue, sponsors, prizes, and judging
- **Workshop planning** — Tips for running hands-on technical workshops with sponsor tools
- **Demo Day planning** — How to set up a demo day showcasing builder projects

<Tip>Start with a Hack Night for your first event — it's the simplest format to organize and a great way to build momentum in your community.</Tip>

## Best practices

Proven strategies from experienced Squad Leaders:

- **Sponsor outreach** — How to identify, contact, and close sponsor deals in your region
- **Community building** — Growing a recurring attendee base through consistent events and engagement
- **Event promotion** — Using social media, developer communities, and local networks to drive registrations
- **Venue selection** — What to look for in an event space (capacity, Wi-Fi, power outlets, accessibility)

## Marketing templates

Pre-built templates to help you promote your events:

- **Social media posts** — Ready-to-customize templates for Twitter, LinkedIn, and other platforms
- **Email outreach** — Templates for sponsor outreach and attendee communications
- **Event descriptions** — Sample event descriptions for different formats

## Getting help

If you need additional support or have questions about the Squad Leader program:

- **Email** — Reach the HackerSquad team at [support@hackersquad.io](mailto:support@hackersquad.io)
- **Squad Leader community** — Connect with other Squad Leaders to share tips and experiences

<Note>Resources are updated regularly as new guides and templates become available. Check back periodically for new materials.</Note>
