title: "Sponsoring Events"
description: "How to purchase a sponsorship and support developer events on HackerSquad"
---

Sponsoring events on HackerSquad puts your developer tools directly in front of builders who are actively coding. This guide walks you through selecting a sponsorship tier, completing payment, and managing your sponsorship.

<Note>You must have a company profile set up before you can sponsor events. See [Company Setup](/company-setup) to create your profile.</Note>

## Sponsorship purchase flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find an event to sponsor">
    Browse the [Event Marketplace](/company-portal/event-marketplace) and click on an event you want to sponsor. Review the event details, expected attendance, and organizer information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a sponsorship tier">
    On the event's sponsorship page, review the available tiers (Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver, Bronze). Each tier lists its specific benefits and pricing. Select the tier that fits your budget and goals.

    Benefits typically include:
    - Logo placement on event materials
    - Speaking or demo opportunities
    - Booth or table at the venue
    - Social media mentions
    - Access to attendee data (with consent)
    - Prize sponsorship opportunities
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your selection">
    Confirm your selected tier and review the full list of benefits included. Verify the pricing and ensure your company profile is up to date, as your company name and logo will be displayed as the sponsor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment via Stripe">
    Click **Purchase Sponsorship** to proceed to the Stripe checkout page. Enter your payment details and complete the transaction. You will receive a confirmation email once the payment is processed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your sponsorship">
    After payment, the sponsored event appears on your [Events Overview](/company-portal/events-overview) page under **Events You're Sponsoring**. You can view your tier, benefits, and event details at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Sponsor events early to secure higher-tier placements. Diamond and Platinum slots are limited and fill quickly for popular events.</Tip>

## After sponsoring

Once your sponsorship is confirmed, you can:

- **View event details** — See the event page with your company listed as a sponsor at your tier level
- **Track payment status** — Your sponsorship shows as Pending, Paid, or Cancelled in your events overview
- **Coordinate with the organizer** — Reach out to the event organizer for logistics like booth setup, speaking slots, or prize delivery
- **Monitor deliverables** — Check what sponsorship benefits have been fulfilled as the event approaches

## Managing sponsorships

All your active and past sponsorships are listed on the [Events Overview](/company-portal/events-overview) page. Each sponsored event displays:

- Event name and date
- Your sponsorship tier
- Payment status
- Event organizer contact

<Warning>Sponsorship purchases are processed through Stripe and are subject to the event organizer's refund policy. Contact the event organizer directly for cancellation requests.</Warning>
