## Sign up

HackerSquad uses OAuth for authentication. Head to [hackersquad.io](https://hackersquad.io) and sign in with one of the supported providers:

- **GitHub** — recommended for developers
- **Google** — sign in with your Google account
- **LinkedIn** — great for professional networking context

<Tip>If you sign in with multiple providers using the same email address, your accounts are automatically linked into a single HackerSquad identity.</Tip>

## For companies

Get your company set up and run your first developer event.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your company profile">
    After signing in, navigate to the **Company Portal** and click **Create Company**. Fill in your company name, upload a logo, add a description, and enter your website URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select a subscription tier that fits your needs. The **Free** plan lets you create up to 3 events per month. Upgrade to **Starter**, **Pro**, **Scale**, or **Enterprise** for more events, Cloud IDE hours, and advanced features. See [Plans & Pricing](/plans-and-pricing) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first event">
    Go to **Events** in the Company Portal and click **Create Event**. Set the event name, date and time, location, format (Hack Night, Hackathon, Workshop, etc.), and add a registration link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your dashboard">
    Your **Company Dashboard** shows total events, upcoming events, submission stats, and quick access to DevRel tools like the Social Content Generator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Complete your company profile before creating events — a verified profile builds trust with builders and sponsors.</Tip>

## For builders

Discover events, register, and start building.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse events">
    Visit the [Events directory](https://hackersquad.io/events) to explore upcoming hack nights, hackathons, workshops, and more. Filter by format, date, or location to find events near you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for an event">
    Click on an event and register as a **Hacker** (if you plan to build a project) or a **Spectator** (to observe and network). Hackers can share their project idea, tech stack, and whether they're looking for a team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your project">
    During the event, submit your project with a name, description, GitHub link, and demo details. Select the sponsor technologies you used and request a demo slot if available.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Register as a **Hacker** if you plan to build and submit a project. Register as a **Spectator** to observe and network.</Tip>

## For Squad Leaders

Squad Leaders are community organizers who run HackerSquad events in their region and earn revenue from sponsorships.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply to the program">
    Visit the [Squad Leader application page](https://hackersquad.io/squad-leaders) and complete the application form with your background, region, and community vision.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get approved">
    The HackerSquad team reviews applications and notifies you by email. Once approved, you gain access to the Squad Leader dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For the full application guide and program details, see [Squad Leader Overview](/squad-leaders/overview).
