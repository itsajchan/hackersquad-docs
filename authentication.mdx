## Sign in with OAuth

HackerSquad uses OAuth for authentication. Visit [hackersquad.io](https://hackersquad.io) and click **Sign In** to get started. You can authenticate with any of the following providers:

- **GitHub** — recommended for developers, links your GitHub profile for project submissions
- **Google** — sign in with your Google account
- **LinkedIn** — great for professional networking context

No passwords to remember — just select your preferred provider and authorize HackerSquad.

## Account linking

If you sign in with multiple providers that share the same email address, HackerSquad automatically links them into a single account. For example, if you first sign in with GitHub and later sign in with Google using the same email, both providers connect to the same HackerSquad identity.

This means:

- Your events, projects, and profile stay consistent regardless of which provider you use to sign in
- You can switch between providers at any time without losing data
- All roles and permissions are tied to your account, not to a specific provider

<Note>Account linking is based on email address. If your OAuth providers use different email addresses, they will create separate HackerSquad accounts.</Note>

## User roles

Every HackerSquad account can hold one or more roles. Your role determines which parts of the platform you can access:

| Role | Description | How it's assigned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Builder** | Default role for all users. Browse events, register, submit projects, and participate in voting. | Automatically assigned on sign-up |
| **Company Manager** | Manage a company profile, create events, access the Company Portal and DevRel tools. | Assigned when you create or are invited to a company |
| **Event Manager** | Full event management access including registrations, judging, speakers, and sponsorships. | Assigned when you create an event or are added as a co-manager |
| **Squad Leader** | Run events in your region, manage a sponsor pipeline, and earn revenue from sponsorships. | Assigned after your Squad Leader application is approved |

Roles are additive — a single account can be a Builder, Company Manager, and Squad Leader at the same time. You don't need separate accounts for different activities on the platform.
