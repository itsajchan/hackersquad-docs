Many events encourage or require team participation. HackerSquad provides tools to create teams, invite members, and link your team to project submissions.

## Creating a team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event page">
    Navigate to the event you're attending and go to the **Teams** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new team">
    Click **Create Team** and give your team a name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Enter the email addresses of the builders you want to invite. They'll receive an email invitation to join your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Register with the **"Looking for team"** option during event registration if you want to find teammates at the event.</Tip>

## Team roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| **Team leader** | The person who created the team. Can invite and remove members, and manage team settings. |
| **Team member** | Accepted an invitation to join. Can view team details and collaborate on projects. |

The team leader is typically responsible for submitting the project on behalf of the team, but any member can edit the submission once it's created.

## Managing invitations

When you're invited to a team, you'll receive an email notification. You can:

- **Accept** the invitation to join the team
- **Reject** the invitation if you're joining a different team or working solo

Pending invitations are visible to the team leader, who can resend or revoke them at any time.

## Linking teams to projects

When submitting a project, add your teammates' email addresses in the **Team members** field. This links the project to all team members so that:

- All members are credited on the project submission
- Judges can see the full team roster
- Prize awards apply to the entire team

See [Project Submissions](/builders/project-submissions) for the full submission workflow.

<Note>Only one project submission is needed per team. The team leader or any member can create and edit it.</Note>
