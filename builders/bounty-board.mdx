The Bounty Board is where companies and event organizers post developer challenges with rewards. Browse available bounties, filter by your skills, and submit solutions to earn payouts.

## Browsing bounties

The Bounty Board lists all available developer bounties. Each bounty includes:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | A short name describing the challenge |
| **Description** | Details about what needs to be built or solved |
| **Skills / Technology** | The technologies or skills required to complete the bounty |
| **Reward** | The payout amount for a successful submission |
| **Status** | Whether the bounty is open, in progress, or completed |

## Filtering bounties

Use filters to find bounties that match your expertise:

- **Skills / Technology** — Filter by programming languages, frameworks, or tools you're proficient in
- **Reward amount** — Sort by payout to find high-value opportunities
- **Status** — Show only open bounties that are accepting submissions

## Submitting a solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a bounty">
    Browse the board and click on a bounty that interests you to view the full requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the requirements">
    Read the description, required technologies, and acceptance criteria carefully before starting work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your solution">
    Complete the challenge following the bounty requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your work">
    Submit your solution with any required deliverables (code repository, demo link, documentation) as specified in the bounty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Read the bounty requirements thoroughly before starting. Submissions that closely match the acceptance criteria are more likely to be approved.</Tip>

## Tracking earnings

You can track your bounty earnings from your profile. Completed and approved bounty submissions show the reward amount and payout status.
