Once you've found an event you want to attend, the next step is to register. HackerSquad offers two registration roles — **Hacker** and **Spectator** — each tailored to a different type of participation.

<Tip>Register as a **Hacker** if you plan to build and submit a project. Register as a **Spectator** to observe, network, and support the builders.</Tip>

## Registration roles

| Role | Who it's for | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hacker** | Developers, designers, and builders who plan to create a project | Submit projects, request demos, use Cloud IDE, form teams, compete for prizes |
| **Spectator** | Community members, recruiters, and supporters | Observe presentations, participate in community voting, network with builders |

## How to register

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the event page">
    Navigate to the event from the [Events Directory](/builders/finding-events) and click the **Register** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your role">
    Choose whether you're registering as a **Hacker** or **Spectator**. This determines which fields you'll fill out and what features you can access during the event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Complete the registration form. The fields depend on your selected role (see below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your registration">
    Review your information and submit. You'll receive a confirmation and can view your registration status on the event page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hacker registration fields

When registering as a Hacker, you'll be asked for:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Project idea** | Optional | A brief description of what you plan to build |
| **Tech stack** | Optional | The technologies or languages you plan to use |
| **Looking for team** | Optional | Toggle this on if you'd like to be matched with other builders |

<Tip>Filling in your project idea and tech stack helps event organizers and other builders find you for team formation.</Tip>

## Spectator registration fields

When registering as a Spectator, you'll be asked for:

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Goals** | Optional | What you hope to get out of attending (e.g., networking, scouting talent, learning) |

## Check-in at the event

On event day, check in at the registration desk or through the event page. Check-in confirms your attendance and unlocks event features:

- **Hackers** — Access to project submission, Cloud IDE (if enabled), demo requests, and team features
- **Spectators** — Access to community voting (if enabled) and live demo viewing

<Note>Event organizers can see check-in status in real time. Make sure to check in so you're counted as an attendee.</Note>

## Looking for a team?

If you toggled **Looking for team** during registration, other attendees and organizers can see that you're available. This is a great way to find collaborators if you don't already have a team.

Once you've connected with teammates, your team leader can formally create a team and invite members. See [Teams](/builders/teams) for details.
