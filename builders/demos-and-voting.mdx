Demos and community voting let you showcase your work and support other builders at HackerSquad events. Present a live demo via WebRTC streaming to judges and attendees, or cast votes for your favorite projects during community voting.

## Viewing demo submissions

From the event page, you can browse all projects that have requested a demo. Each demo entry shows:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Project name** | The project being demoed |
| **Team** | The builder or team presenting |
| **Status** | Current status: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected**, or **Live** |
| **Description** | A summary of what the project does |

## Requesting a live demo

If you want to present your project to the event audience, you can request a live demo slot. Live demos use WebRTC streaming directly from your browser — no extra software is needed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit your project">
    Make sure you've [submitted your project](/builders/project-submissions) and toggled the **Request a demo** option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Event organizers review demo requests and approve or reject them. You'll be notified when your request is reviewed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare to present">
    Once approved, you'll appear in the presentation queue. Make sure your project is ready to demonstrate and your browser has camera/microphone access if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your stream">
    When it's your turn, start your WebRTC stream from the event page. Your screen and audio will be broadcast to the live demo viewer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Present your project">
    Walk through your project, explain what you built, and demonstrate how it works. Keep it concise and focused on the key highlights.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Practice your demo beforehand. A clear, focused 3-5 minute walkthrough is more effective than a long, unstructured presentation.</Tip>

<Note>Both you and the viewers need a modern browser with WebRTC support (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). No plugins or downloads are required.</Note>

## The live demo experience

During your live demo presentation:

- **Real-time streaming** — your screen and audio stream directly to the event display via WebRTC
- **Low latency** — the stream is near-instant, suitable for live interaction with the audience
- **Recording** — event organizers may record your demo, which will be linked to your project submission afterward

## Community voting

When the event organizer enables community voting, registered attendees can vote on their favorite projects. Voting adds a community-driven signal alongside judge scores.

### How to vote

1. **Browse projects** — view all submitted projects on the event page
2. **Cast your votes** — click the vote button on projects you want to support
3. **Stay within limits** — the system enforces per-user and per-project vote limits set by the event organizer

| Limit | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Max votes per user** | The total number of votes you can cast across all projects |
| **Max votes per project** | The maximum votes you can give to any single project |

<Tip>A typical event allows 5 total votes with a maximum of 1 vote per project. Spread your votes across the projects you found most impressive.</Tip>

### When voting is available

Community voting is controlled by the event organizer. It may be enabled:

- **During the event** — vote as projects are submitted and demoed
- **After demos** — vote after seeing all live presentations
- **For a limited window** — voting may open and close at specific times

Check the event page for the current voting status.

## Viewing the leaderboard

The leaderboard shows project rankings based on judge scores and community votes:

- **Rankings** — see where each project stands overall
- **Judge scores** — scores assigned by the judging panel
- **Community votes** — vote counts from attendee voting
- **Combined ranking** — the overall position factoring both signals

The leaderboard updates in real time as judges score and votes come in. Check it throughout the event to track how your project is performing.
