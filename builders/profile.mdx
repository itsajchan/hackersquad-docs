Your builder profile is your identity on HackerSquad. It shows other attendees, organizers, and sponsors who you are and how to connect with you.

## Editing your profile

To update your profile, navigate to your account settings. You can edit the following:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Your display name shown on project submissions and event registrations |
| **Phone** | Your phone number (optional, not publicly visible) |
| **Avatar** | A profile photo uploaded from your device |
| **LinkedIn** | Your LinkedIn profile URL |
| **GitHub** | Your GitHub profile URL |
| **Discord** | Your Discord username |
| **Twitter** | Your Twitter/X handle |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar or name in the navigation bar and select **Profile** or **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your information">
    Edit your name, phone number, and social links. Changes are saved as you update each field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an avatar">
    Click the avatar area to upload a profile photo. This image appears on your project submissions, team listings, and event registrations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Adding your GitHub and LinkedIn profiles makes it easier for sponsors and other builders to connect with you after events.</Tip>

## Social links

Social links appear on your public profile and are visible to other event attendees, organizers, and sponsors. Adding these links helps you:

- **Network** with other builders at events
- **Get noticed** by sponsors whose tools you used in your projects
- **Build credibility** by linking to your GitHub contributions and professional profile

## Viewing your profile

You can preview how your profile looks to other users from the profile settings page. This shows your name, avatar, and any social links you've added.
