Builders are the heart of HackerSquad. Whether you're a developer, designer, or maker, HackerSquad gives you the tools to discover events, build projects, demo your work, and connect with the community.

## What you can do

As a builder on HackerSquad, you can:

- **Discover events** — Browse hackathons, hack nights, workshops, demo days, and more in the [Events Directory](/builders/finding-events)
- **Register and attend** — Sign up as a Hacker or Spectator with a streamlined [registration process](/builders/registration)
- **Submit projects** — Showcase what you build with GitHub links, demo descriptions, and technology tags via [Project Submissions](/builders/project-submissions)
- **Use the Cloud IDE** — Code directly in your browser with a full VS Code environment powered by [Cloud IDE](/builders/cloud-ide)
- **Present live demos** — Stream your project demo to the audience using WebRTC via [Demos & Voting](/builders/demos-and-voting)
- **Vote on projects** — Participate in community voting when enabled by event organizers
- **Track your process** — Document your engineering journey with [Engineering Notebooks](/builders/engineering-notebooks)
- **Form teams** — Create or join [teams](/builders/teams) to collaborate during events
- **Manage your profile** — Set up your [builder profile](/builders/profile) with social links and a bio
- **Earn bounties** — Browse and complete developer bounties on the [Bounty Board](/builders/bounty-board)
- **Win prizes** — Compete for prizes awarded by judges, community votes, or raffle

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [hackersquad.io](https://hackersquad.io) using GitHub, Google, or LinkedIn. See [Authentication](/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find an event">
    Browse upcoming events in the [Events Directory](/builders/finding-events) and find one that matches your interests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Register as a **Hacker** (if you plan to build) or a **Spectator** (if you want to observe and network). See [Registration](/builders/registration).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and submit">
    During the event, work on your project and [submit it](/builders/project-submissions) with a description, GitHub link, and demo details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Demo and compete">
    Present your work, participate in [community voting](/builders/demos-and-voting), and check the leaderboard for results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
