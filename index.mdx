HackerSquad is the all-in-one platform for running developer events, engaging builders with sponsor tools, and growing technical communities. From hackathons to demo days, HackerSquad provides event management, a Cloud IDE, real-time demos, and sponsor engagement tools to make every event a success and a way to capture every meaningful metric.
